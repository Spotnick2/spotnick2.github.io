AltStable Companion: Privacy policy

AltStable Companion is a free, open-source Windows app made by one person (Spotnick2) for the World of Warcraft: Forever addon AltStable. It runs on your PC. There is no server of ours: the developer receives nothing from the app, ever - no account, no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reports.

This page says what the app reads, keeps and sends. The README says the same with more detail, feature by feature.

What it reads on your PC

  • AltStable's saved files in your game folder (WTF\Account\...\SavedVariables): your characters, as the addon recorded them.
  • The game's screenshots that match a portrait capture.
  • With enhanced portraits on, the pictures in Codex's own generated_images folder (%USERPROFILE%\.codex\generated_images, or under CODEX_HOME), to pick up the one Codex just made.

What it keeps, and where

Only on your PC:

  • %APPDATA%\AltStableCompanion: its settings, its log, the armory and enhanced pictures it keeps for each character, and - only if you set them up - your Blizzard API client and your Google sign-in. Those two are encrypted for your Windows user (Windows DPAPI), never written to the log, and never included in a diagnostics file. With the Google Sheet, the settings also hold your spreadsheet's ID and address.
  • The game's Interface\AddOns\AltStableCutouts folder: the portraits it makes for the game (.tga pictures, each with a small .json file describing it).
  • Your Downloads folder: a diagnostics file, only when you press Help → Save diagnostics…. It is a text file for a bug report, and you choose whether to send it.
  • Codex's generated_images folder: Codex itself keeps every picture it makes for enhanced portraits there. The app only reads that folder.

What leaves your PC

Nothing, except for the features you turn on or press:

Feature Sent to What
Portraits from the armory render.worldofwarcraft.com (Blizzard) a request for each character's picture, by the number in its GUID
Your own Blizzard API client oauth.battle.net, us.api.blizzard.com (Blizzard) your client's ID and secret, to the first only, for a token; then one request per character, naming it
Enhanced portraits the Codex CLI on your PC, which sends it to OpenAI under the ChatGPT account it is signed in to each portrait to redraw, with a prompt naming the character's gender, race and class and the art style you chose
Check for updates api.github.com, github.com a request for this project's list of releases; the new version's file when you press Update now
Google Sheet Google (see below) your characters' data, into your own spreadsheet

Google Sheet and your Google data

The Google Sheet export is off until you press Connect in the app's settings and sign in with Google.

  • What the app may access. It asks for one permission, drive.file: the files the app itself creates in your Google Drive. It cannot see, read or change any other file in your Drive.
  • What it reads from Google.
    • From the spreadsheet it created, before each export: the ID and title of each tab, tabs you added included, and the labels it put on its own tabs, so it rewrites its own tabs and leaves yours alone.
    • From your Drive, when the address it kept no longer works (a new PC, a reinstall, the spreadsheet deleted): the IDs of the spreadsheets it created, and whether each is in the trash, so it can write the same one again instead of making another.
  • What it writes. One spreadsheet, named AltStable, holding your characters as AltStable recorded them: name, class, faction, level, item level, rested XP, gold, last played, hearthstone, guild, race, realm and its ruleset, professions and skills, reputations, equipped items and enchants. Accounts appear as 1, 2, ...; your account names stay on your PC. The spreadsheet is yours, in your Drive, shared with nobody unless you share it.
  • When. When you press Connect, Export now or Disconnect, and on its own: Export on its own is ticked when you connect, so by default the app exports 15 minutes after the game has closed and stayed closed, when AltStable has saved something new. The delay is yours to change (5 to 120 minutes), and unticking it leaves only the exports you ask for.
  • Where it goes. The app sends it only to Google: oauth2.googleapis.com (the sign-in), sheets.googleapis.com (the spreadsheet) and www.googleapis.com (Drive, to find the spreadsheet it made). Nothing goes to the developer or anyone else.
  • The spreadsheet's pictures. The spreadsheet shows pictures by their web address: the section icons, from this project's GitHub page (raw.githubusercontent.com), and, with armory portraits on, each character's armory picture or its race's silhouette, from render.worldofwarcraft.com, whose address holds the number in the character's GUID. Google fetches them, not the app, and only once you open the spreadsheet and choose Allow access there, which Google asks for once.
  • What is kept. The sign-in (a refresh token), encrypted on your PC as said above, and the spreadsheet's ID and address in the app's settings.

AltStable Companion's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Your Google data is used only to write your spreadsheet; it is not sold, not used for advertising, not shared with anyone, not read by any person, and not used to train any AI model.

Removing it

  • Disconnect in the app deletes the sign-in from your PC and asks Google to cancel it. It keeps the spreadsheet's address, so connecting the same Google account again goes on writing the same spreadsheet. You can also remove the app's access at any time from your Google Account, under Third-party apps & services.

  • The spreadsheet stays yours: delete it from your Drive whenever you like.

  • To remove everything the app keeps, quit it, then delete:

    • %APPDATA%\AltStableCompanion (settings, log, pictures, the spreadsheet's address and any sign-in);
    • the AltStableCutouts folder in your game's Interface\AddOns folder (the portraits it made for the game);
    • any diagnostics file you saved in Downloads (AltStableCompanion-diagnostics-...).

    Codex's generated_images folder belongs to Codex; the app never deletes from it. Delete the pictures there yourself if you no longer want them.
